What documents are required for a gold loan? ▼
You only need a valid government-issued ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID), address proof, and your gold. Muthoot Finance makes the process completely hassle-free with minimal documentation.
What is the LTV ratio and why does it matter? ▼
LTV (Loan-to-Value) ratio is the percentage of your gold's value you can borrow. Muthoot offers up to 75-80% LTV, meaning if your gold is worth ₹1 lakh, you can borrow ₹75,000-80,000. Higher LTV means you get more money against your gold.
Can I repay the loan early without penalty? ▼
Yes! Muthoot Finance allows early repayment without any penalty charges. You can repay the entire loan amount at any time during the loan period.
How is my gold stored and is it insured? ▼
Your gold is stored in secure, air-conditioned vaults with CCTV surveillance. Complete insurance coverage is provided at no extra cost. Your gold is completely safe with Muthoot Finance.
What if I cannot repay on time? ▼
Muthoot Finance offers flexible repayment options. If you're unable to repay, you can request a tenure extension or restructure your loan. The interest will continue to accrue, but they work with customers to find suitable solutions.
Is the interest rate fixed or variable? ▼
Interest rates are fixed for the loan tenure. However, rates may vary based on loan amount and tenor. Current rates start from 8.15% p.a., but it's best to check with your nearest branch for exact rates applicable to your loan.
