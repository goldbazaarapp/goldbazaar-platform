01 Overview

GoldBazaar is committed to providing a fair, transparent, and effective mechanism for addressing user and vendor complaints. Your feedback helps us improve our services.

This policy applies to all registered users, customers, and vendors on the GoldBazaar platform. Complaints are handled in accordance with applicable Indian consumer protection laws and the IT Act, 2000.

02 Types of Complaints

We accept grievances related to:

  • Service quality: Poor experience with a vendor listed on GoldBazaar
  • Transaction disputes: Payment issues, overcharging, or unfulfilled commitments
  • Platform issues: Technical problems, errors, or account-related issues
  • Data & privacy: Concerns about how your personal data is handled
  • Vendor conduct: Fraud, misrepresentation, or harassment by a listed vendor
  • Content concerns: Misleading listings or inaccurate information on the platform

03 How to Submit a Complaint

You may raise a grievance through any of the following channels:

1
Email Us
Send details to hello@goldbazaar.app with subject "Grievance: [Your Issue]"
2
Call Us
Speak to our support team at (Mon–Sat, 9 AM–6 PM)
3
Contact Form
Use the Contact Us form on our homepage and select "Grievance" as type

Information to include

  • Your full name and registered mobile number
  • Description of the issue with relevant dates
  • Order or transaction reference (if applicable)
  • Name of vendor involved (if applicable)
  • Supporting evidence (screenshots, receipts)

04 Resolution Timeline

Day 1 — Acknowledgement
We acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 1 business day and issue a reference number.
Day 3 — Initial Review
Our support team reviews your complaint and may contact you for additional information.
Day 7 — Resolution or Update
We aim to resolve most complaints within 7 business days. Complex cases receive a detailed status update.
Day 30 — Final Resolution
All complaints are resolved or formally closed with a written outcome within 30 days.
