Mission

Making Gold Simple & Accessible

Gold is India's most beloved asset — held in 77% of Indian households. Yet accessing gold services remains fragmented, opaque, and often exploitative. GoldBazaar exists to change that.

We bring transparency, trust, and technology to every gold transaction — whether you're getting a loan, selling inherited jewellery, or building long-term wealth through digital gold.
