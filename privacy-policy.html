01 Introduction

Imprimis Innovations Pvt. Ltd ("GoldBazaar", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains how we handle personal data when you use our website and services.

By using our Platform, you agree to the practices described here. This Policy is governed by applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

02 Data We Collect

Information you provide

  • Identity: Full name, date of birth, photograph for KYC
  • Contact: Mobile number, email address, city
  • Financial: Gold ownership details, loan requirements (shared only with your consent)
  • Business (Vendors): Business name, category, GSTIN, address
  • Enquiries: Messages submitted via contact or onboarding forms

Automatically collected

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, search queries
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Location: City-level location from IP (no GPS without explicit consent)

03 How We Use It

  • Providing and personalising our services (gold loans, buy/sell, marketplace listings)
  • Sending transactional notifications — OTPs, booking confirmations, status updates
  • Responding to your enquiries and customer support requests
  • Verifying vendor identity and managing onboarding
  • Improving Platform performance and fixing issues
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Sending promotional offers — only where you have opted in

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

04 Sharing & Disclosure

💎 Vendors & Lenders

Your enquiry details are shared only with the specific vendor or lender you choose, to fulfil your request.

⚙️ Service Providers

Technology vendors, cloud hosting, SMS/OTP gateways — bound by data processing agreements and permitted to process data only as instructed by us.

⚖️ Legal Requirements

We may disclose data to courts, law enforcement, or regulators (RBI, SEBI) when required by applicable law.

05 Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  • Access controls: Staff access is restricted on a need-to-know basis with multi-factor authentication.
  • Payments: Card data is handled by PCI-DSS certified payment gateways — we do not store raw card numbers.
  • Incidents: In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by law.

If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, contact us immediately at hello@goldbazaar.app.

06 Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected or as required by law. In general:

  • Account data is retained for the duration of your account plus 3 years after closure.
  • KYC and transaction records are kept for 5–8 years as mandated by RBI and PMLA regulations.
  • Contact enquiries and support records are kept for 2 years.
  • Marketing consent records are retained until consent is withdrawn.

Upon expiry of the retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

07 Children's Privacy

Our Platform is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has submitted data to us without parental consent, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
